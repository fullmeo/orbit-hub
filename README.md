## Social Media Integration (v0.4)

Orbit-hub makes it easy to showcase and track your social presence:

### Recommended Setup
- Prominent social icons on homepage and Connect page
- Automatic click tracking via GA4
- Link your Orbit-hub in your Instagram/Facebook/TikTok bio

### Best Platforms to Link
1. Instagram (priority)
2. Facebook
3. YouTube
4. TikTok
5. Spotify / Apple Music (already in music section)

Add your handles in `connect.html` and optionally feature them on the homepage.
