About Togbé Adjos

Vodun Jazz Pioneer | Cultural Ambassador | Ouidah, Benin

Artist Biography

Togbé Adjos is a vocalist, songwriter, and bandleader based in Ouidah, the spiritual centre of Benin's Vodun culture. Over 20 years of musical innovation, Adjos has pioneered Vodun Jazz — a revolutionary fusion combining ancient West African Vodun percussion with modern global sounds including reggae, jazz, and afrobeat.

A close friend and protégé of legendary musician Stan Tohon, Adjos dedicates his work to preserving and celebrating Vodun traditions while introducing them to international audiences. His music explores themes of spirituality, cultural identity, and the bridge between tradition and modernity.

His latest album, Maman Tchamba (2020), takes listeners deep into Vodun spirituality, blending dub reggae, jazz, and Beninese traditions. Beyond his solo career, Adjos founded and organizes the annual Vodun Jazz Festival, bringing together musicians from Brazil, Haiti, the USA, and across the global Vodun diaspora to celebrate their shared cultural roots.

Musical Style & Influences

🥁 Ancient Vodun Percussion

The foundation of Togbé's sound draws from centuries-old Beninese Vodun rhythms and sacred percussion traditions that echo the spiritual roots of West African culture.

🎺 Jazz Fusion Innovation

Complex harmonies and improvisational structures merge ancient polyrhythmic grooves with modern jazz sensibilities, creating a unique sonic landscape that bridges tradition and contemporary expression.

🎤 Reggae & Dub Elements

Reggae rhythms and dub production techniques add depth and contemporary appeal, transforming sacred traditions into music that resonates with global audiences while honoring ancestral roots.

🌍 Afrobeat & Diaspora Fusion

Togbé incorporates afrobeat polyrhythms and collaborates with musicians from Brazil, Haiti, and across the diaspora, creating a truly global expression of Vodun cultural identity.

Notable Collaborations & Mentorship

Stan Tohon

Legendary Mentor & Musical Pioneer

A close friend and protégé of legendary musician Stan Tohon, Togbé credits Stan's guidance in developing his distinct Vodun Jazz sound. Stan's legacy of bridging traditional Beninese music with global audiences deeply influenced Togbé's artistic direction and commitment to cultural preservation.

Diaspora Musicians Network

International Collaborations

Through the annual Vodun Jazz Festival, Togbé collaborates with musicians from Brazil, Haiti, the USA, and across the global Vodun diaspora. These connections celebrate shared spiritual and cultural roots while creating new musical expressions that honor ancestral traditions.

Beyond Music Platform

International Music Distribution

Partnering with Beyond Music to share Togbé's timeless Vodun Jazz rhythms with international audiences, expanding the reach of Beninese cultural traditions while supporting emerging artists from the diaspora.
