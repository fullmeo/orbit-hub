Annual Vodun Jazz Festival

About the Festival

Togbé Adjos founded the annual Vodun Jazz Festival to create a gathering space for musicians of the African diaspora. The festival celebrates the interconnected spiritual and musical traditions of Vodun communities across the Americas and Africa.

Featuring artists from Brazil (Candomblé), Haiti (Vodou), the United States, and West Africa, this festival honors the continuity of Vodun traditions while celebrating contemporary musical innovation.

Festival Organization

For festival participation, artist applications, sponsorship opportunities, or vendor inquiries, contact:

The festival brings together cultural organizations, musicians, and community members. Collaborative opportunities welcome.

Sister Festival

The Vodun Jazz Festival works closely with the Ouidah Blues, Jazz & Gospel Festival, celebrating the musical and spiritual heritage of Ouidah, Benin.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I request Togbé for a performance or festival?

Email Adjos67@gmail.com with event details (date, location, venue type, audience size, and expected duration). International performances typically require 60-90 days of advance planning to coordinate schedules and logistics.

How can diaspora musicians collaborate with Togbé?

Send collaboration proposals to Adjos67@gmail.com. Include information about your musical style, project vision, previous work (links/videos if available), and your connection to Vodun or African diaspora traditions. Collaborations spanning Brazil, Haiti, the US, and Africa are especially welcome.

When is the annual Vodun Jazz Festival?

Festival dates are announced each year. Subscribe to the mailing list or follow social media for announcements. Contact Adjos67@gmail.com for festival participation details, including artist applications, vendor opportunities, and sponsorship information.

Do you accept interview and media requests?

Yes! Send press inquiries to Adjos67@gmail.com. Include publication/outlet name, interview format (written, video, podcast, etc.), topic focus (music, cultural preservation, festival, spirituality), and your deadline. Press materials and high-resolution photos available upon request.

How often does Togbé release new music?

Subscribe to the mailing list for release announcements. Follow social media platforms for behind-the-scenes updates, live performance clips, and cultural content between album releases. Current discography is available on Bandcamp, Apple Music, and Shazam.

How can I support Togbé's work?

Listen and share music on streaming platforms (Bandcamp, Apple Music, Shazam), follow on social media, attend live performances and festivals, purchase music and merchandise, engage with cultural content, collaborate if you're a diaspora musician, and help preserve Vodun traditions in your community.

What is the connection to the Ouidah Festival?

Togbé Adjos is deeply connected to Ouidah, Benin, where the Ouidah Blues, Jazz & Gospel Festival takes place. The festivals are sister events celebrating African musical and spiritual heritage. Both festivals honor traditions and connect global audiences to the spiritual center of Vodun culture.

Can I book Togbé for recording sessions or studio collaborations?

Studio and recording collaborations are welcome. Email Adjos67@gmail.com with project details, musical goals, timeline, and budget information. Describe how your project connects to or honors Vodun traditions, African diaspora culture, or spiritual themes.
