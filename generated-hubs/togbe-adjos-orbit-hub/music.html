Maman Tchamba

Maman Tchamba is a transformative spiritual journey through Vodun Jazz, exploring ancient Vodun traditions, reggae dub, jazz sophistication, and Beninese musical heritage. Released in 2020, this album represents Togbé Adjos's artistic evolution and deepest exploration of ancestral African spirituality merged with contemporary musical innovation.

Musical Vision: A fusion of sacred Vodun percussion rhythms, dub reggae aesthetics, jazz harmonies, and the wisdom of Beninese traditions. Maman Tchamba honors the spiritual guidance of feminine ancestors and the earth mother (Maman in Fon language).

Featured Tracks

Spiritual Journeys

Maman Tchamba features three iconic tracks that represent the spiritual core of Togbé Adjos's artistic vision. Each composition honors a different aspect of Vodun spirituality and the diaspora's African heritage.

Sakpata

Ancient Vodun rhythms merged with sophisticated jazz progression. This composition invokes Sakpata, the orisha protector and healer, honoring the connection between ancestral wisdom and contemporary musical expression.

Gambada

Infectious Beninese percussion rhythms layered with reggae dub aesthetics. Gambada is a celebration of movement, joy, and the ancestral connections that bind the African diaspora. Infectious grooves meet spiritual depth.

Lêgba

A subtle, intricate tribute to Lêgba, the crossroads keeper and messenger between worlds. Jazz-inflected melodies create space for contemplation, honoring the complexity of cultural bridges and spiritual transitions.
